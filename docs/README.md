# English

## What is this folder for?

This folder contains the human-readable documents that explain **how the pilot was designed before looking at the technical evidence**.

Think of it as the experiment's instruction book.

**In simple terms:**

> **`docs/` explains the method.  
> `method/` will contain the executable tools.  
> `evidence/` will contain what actually happened during the runs.**

The documents in this folder describe the research design, the declared service framework, the mystery-shopper procedure, the agent prompts and the analysis matrix used around the reference experiment.

---

## Documents

### `01_Research design.docx`

Research-design document for the test-purchase experiment.

It defines the methodological frame of the pilot and the logic used to observe an AI service interaction in a controlled environment.

Its role is to explain **what is being tested and why**.

### `02_Service documents.docx`

Reference service documents used to define the expected service environment.

They provide the declared service context against which the interaction can be interpreted.

Their role is to establish **what the service is supposed to be** before evaluating what the agent actually does.

### `03_Mystery shopper worksheet.docx`

Operational worksheet for the mystery-shopper / test-purchase procedure.

It helps structure the observation of the service interaction and the collection of relevant evidence.

Its role is to make the test **repeatable and inspectable**.

### `04_Agent prompts.docx`

Consolidated document containing the prompts used for the different AI roles involved in the experiment.

The source package also contains the individual prompt files used for:

- AI Policy;
- AI Service Passport;
- AI Receipt Template;
- Service Agent;
- Buyer;
- Analyst.

The role of this document is to make the declared AI instructions visible and reviewable.

### `Analysis matrix (demo).xlsx`

Demonstration analysis matrix associated with the methodology.

It provides a structured way to organize and review observations produced during the experiment.

It should be read as a supporting methodological artifact, not as the authoritative runtime evidence itself.

---

## How the pieces fit together

At a high level:

    Research design
          ↓
    Declared service documents
          ↓
    Agent prompts
          ↓
    Mystery-shopper interaction
          ↓
    Runtime execution
          ↓
    Receipt + journal + state
          ↓
    Deterministic validation
          ↓
    NeoMundi runtime observation
          ↓
    Analysis / evidence review

The documentation therefore describes the **rules and setup of the experiment**.

The runtime files generated by the actual runs belong elsewhere in the repository.

---

## Responsibility boundary

The methodology and NeoMundi measurement layer remain distinct.

**aibusiness.vc methodology**
→ defines the test-purchase design, service context, prompts and receipt-conformity approach.

**Execution environment**
→ records what the simulated platform actually executed.

**Deterministic validator**
→ compares the AI-generated receipt with authoritative execution evidence.

**NeoMundi ControlTower**
→ measures the submitted runtime observation and provides traceable measurement outputs.

The documentation should not be read as transferring receipt-conformity authority to NeoMundi.

---

## What these documents are not

These documents are **not**:

- the authoritative execution journal;
- the final platform state;
- the deterministic validation report;
- the NeoMundi audit response;
- proof that an AI receipt is conformant;
- proof of production readiness.

Those claims must be grounded in the actual run artifacts and validation evidence.

---

## Repository relationship

The intended repository separation is:

    docs/
        methodology and explanatory documents

    method/
        executable simulator, harness, validator and prompt sources

    evidence/
        frozen runs, journals, receipts, validation reports and NeoMundi bridge outputs

This separation makes it easier to distinguish:

**what was planned → what was executed → what was verified.**

## Frontière des responsabilités

La méthodologie et la couche de mesure NeoMundi restent distinctes.

**Méthodologie aibusiness.vc**
→ définit le test-purchase, le contexte de service, les prompts et l’approche de conformité du reçu.

**Environnement d’exécution**
→ enregistre ce que la plateforme simulée a réellement exécuté.

**Validateur déterministe**
→ compare le reçu généré par l’IA avec les preuves d’exécution autoritatives.

**NeoMundi ControlTower**
→ mesure l’observation runtime soumise et fournit des sorties de mesure traçables.

La documentation ne doit pas être interprétée comme transférant à NeoMundi l’autorité sur la conformité du reçu.

---

## Ce que ces documents ne sont pas

Ces documents ne sont **pas** :

- le journal d’exécution autoritatif ;
- l’état final de la plateforme ;
- le rapport de validation déterministe ;
- la réponse d’audit NeoMundi ;
- une preuve qu’un reçu IA est conforme ;
- une preuve de préparation à la production.

Ces affirmations doivent être étayées par les artefacts réels des runs et les preuves de validation.

---

## Relation avec le reste du dépôt

La séparation prévue est :

    docs/
        méthodologie et documents explicatifs

    method/
        simulateur, harness, validateur et sources de prompts exécutables

    evidence/
        runs gelés, journaux, reçus, rapports de validation et sorties du bridge NeoMundi

Cette séparation permet de distinguer clairement :

**ce qui était prévu → ce qui a été exécuté → ce qui a été vérifié.**
