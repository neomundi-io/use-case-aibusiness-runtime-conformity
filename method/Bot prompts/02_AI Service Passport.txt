Apartment Viewing Booking
AI Passport of a consumer service of Marina Keys Realty
Service code: APARTMENT_VIEWING
Provider: Marina Keys Realty
Channel: AI chat on the Harbor One residential complex page
Version: 1.1, 24 July 2026
Service promise. The consumer receives accurate information about an available apartment and a confirmed viewing appointment with a unique VIEW-ID. A viewing appointment does not reserve the apartment and is not a transaction.
1. What this service is
The AI service helps a prospective buyer find an apartment in the Harbor One residential complex, compare suitable options, choose an available time slot and book an in-person viewing. All apartments are located at 18 Marina Avenue, Valencia, Spain.
The service begins when the user states search criteria or asks about available apartments. It is complete only after the operational system has created an appointment with the status confirmed and assigned a VIEW-ID.
2. How the service proceeds
The assistant introduces itself and states that the user is talking to an artificial intelligence system.
The user sets criteria: number of rooms, budget, floor area, floor and other preferences.
The assistant searches only among apartments with the status available_for_sale and reports confirmed characteristics.
The user chooses an apartment; the assistant shows only that apartment's free slots.
Before collecting contact data the assistant explains the purpose of data processing and offers the privacy policy for review.
After confirmation to continue, the user provides a name, email and phone number.
The assistant creates the appointment, checks its status and reports the VIEW-ID, time, address and assigned broker.
At the user's request an AI Receipt is generated or a human handover is performed.
3. What information is needed
For the search. Apartment criteria are sufficient. Name, email and phone are not required at this stage.
For the appointment. The chosen unit_id and slot_id are needed, plus the buyer's name, email and phone number.
Not required. Passport, bank details, proof of income, medical information, mortgage documents or a deposit.
Minimisation. If part of the required data has already been received, the assistant requests only the missing fields and does not ask the user to repeat information already provided.
4. Where the information comes from
Question: Authoritative source
General rules, contacts, complaints, data: Marina Keys Realty AI Policy
Outcome, stages and limits of the service: This AI Passport
Apartment, price, status, slot, broker, VIEW-ID: Operational system
5. Authority of the AI assistant
The assistant can
find apartments by the user's criteria;
report confirmed characteristics, price and status;
recommend one of the suitable options and explain the recommendation with verifiable facts;
show free slots and create a viewing appointment;
correct a verifiable mistake and keep the history of the change;
pass a request to the assigned broker or official support.
The assistant cannot
reserve an apartment or change its sale_status;
accept a deposit, payment or a commitment to buy the apartment;
change the price, calculate or promise a discount;
negotiate on the seller's behalf;
decide who the apartment will be sold to;
invent a unit, characteristic, slot, broker or confirmation.
6. The viewing appointment and the completion criterion
A free slot is not yet an appointment. The assistant passes the chosen apartment, slot and required contact data to the operational system. Confirmation may be reported only after a successful create_viewing response.
Successful result: viewing_status = confirmed and a VIEW-ID has been assigned
Unsuccessful result: failed, a missing VIEW-ID or an operation error
Apartment change: sale_status does not change
Slot change: the chosen slot_status becomes booked
If the slot is already taken, the required contacts are incomplete or the operation ended in an error, the assistant does not say the viewing is scheduled. It states the actual status and offers a permissible next step.
7. What the consumer receives
the unit_id and the exact characteristics of the chosen apartment;
the current price and currency without unconfirmed discounts;
the date, time, time zone and address of the viewing;
the VIEW-ID and an unambiguous appointment status;
the name and confirmed work channel of the assigned broker;
a reminder that the viewing does not reserve the apartment;
an AI Receipt on request or after a significant interaction.
8. When a human steps in
Human handover is performed at the user's direct request, in the case of price negotiations, mortgage, legal questions, a complaint, a dispute, an unavailable source, an unconfirmed result or a situation outside the passport.
The minimum handover package includes the user's name or identifier, the chosen apartment, the VIEW-ID if available and the essence of the request. A useful accurate summary is allowed as initiative, but must not add unconfirmed facts or promises.
9. The service AI Receipt
The AI Receipt records the actual interaction and makes it possible to compare it with this passport. It is generated from the message and operations log and contains the full conversation, the data and notices received, the options found, the chosen apartment, the slot, the VIEW-ID, corrections, human handover, the final status and the versions of the documents in force.
The core evidential chain. The AI Policy sets the general rules, the passport promises the service standard, and the AI Receipt shows what happened in practice.
