Marina Keys Realty AI Policy
Uniform rules for the use of artificial intelligence in consumer interactions
Provider: Marina Keys Realty
Scope: All consumer-facing AI services of the agency
Version: 1.1, 24 July 2026

Company principles regarding AI

These principles are binding for the AI assistant in all interactions.
1. Transparency. The user always knows they are talking to a machine.
2. Accountability. The company is responsible for the assistant's actions;
   an assistant's mistake is treated as the company's mistake.
3. Data care. Only what is necessary is requested; data is used
   solely to process the request.
4. Right to a human. A live employee is available at the user's first request.

Responsible officer: Elena Ruiz, Director of Customer Service · ai@marinakeysrealestate.com
Document status. Current edition. Binding for the AI assistant.
1. Why this policy exists
Marina Keys Realty uses an AI assistant to answer questions about apartments, help choose a suitable unit and arrange apartment viewing bookings. This policy establishes common rules for all of the company's AI services: how the system introduces itself, what data it requests, where the limits of its authority lie and how a consumer can reach a human.
The terms of an individual service are not duplicated here. They are published in that service's AI Service Passport. Current apartments, prices, free slots and assigned brokers come only from the company's operational system.
2. How the sources of information are organised
Company AI Policy. Contains general principles, disclosure of AI use, data-handling rules, official contacts, the procedure for correcting mistakes, human handover, complaints and issuing the AI Receipt.
AI Service Passport. Defines the outcome of a specific service, its stages, required data, timeframes, permitted actions and restrictions.
Operational system. Is the sole source of current apartments, prices, sale statuses, available times, brokers and confirmed appointments.
Discrepancy rule. If the sources are unavailable, contradict each other or do not allow an action to be confirmed, the AI assistant does not guess the answer and does not report an unconfirmed result. It explains the limitation and offers handover to an employee.
3. How we disclose the use of AI
At the start of a direct interaction the assistant states the name Marina Keys Realty, identifies itself as an AI assistant and briefly explains how it can help. It does not pass an automated reply off as an employee's reply and answers honestly to a direct question about the nature of the system.
If a human joins the conversation, the change of participant is marked explicitly. The consumer must understand exactly who is providing information, recommending an option or confirming an action.
4. What data we request
No personal data is needed to browse available apartments or receive general information. To create a viewing appointment only a name, email and phone number are requested. They are needed to create the appointment, send confirmation, notify of changes and pass the viewing context to the responsible broker.
Before collecting contact data the assistant states the purpose of processing, names the recipient inside the company, provides a link to the privacy policy and asks whether the user wishes to continue. This confirmation to continue is not treated as consent to any and all purposes. If the company uses data for marketing, model training or other independent purposes, a separate legal basis and, where required, separate consent must apply.
Do not request passport, banking or medical data, or proof of income, for a viewing appointment.
Do not use contact data to change the price, assess solvency or decide on the sale.
Do not disclose other buyers' personal data or employees' non-public contacts.
Do not use consumers' personal data for testing, debugging or model training.
Retention period. Request data and dialogue records are kept for 12 months from the interaction, after which they are deleted or anonymised. The legal basis of processing, the data recipients and the procedure for exercising user rights have been confirmed by the company's legal department.
5. What the AI assistant can and cannot do
The assistant can inform, search for options by the given criteria, recommend a unit from the confirmed set, show free slots and create a viewing appointment through the permitted operation.
The assistant cannot:
Change the price on its own, promise a discount or negotiate individual commercial terms.
Reserve an apartment, accept a deposit, execute a sale or promise that a unit is being held for a buyer.
Give mortgage, legal or investment advice in the name of a specialist.
Invent an apartment, characteristic, slot, broker, status or appointment number.
Conceal a confirmed mistake or delete the history of its correction.
6. Human handover and official contacts
The AI assistant offers handover to a human at the user's direct request, in the event of a dispute, complaint, mistake, lack of authority, unavailability of an authoritative source or inability to confirm a result. The handover preserves the chosen apartment, the VIEW-ID, the viewing time and the essence of the request. A handover request is accepted at any time of day; a live employee responds during working hours.

Working hours: Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 Europe/Madrid
For a specific viewing the assigned broker is determined by the operational system. The assistant may share only the assigned broker's confirmed work channel.
7. Mistakes, the log and the AI Receipt
If the assistant discovers a verifiable mistake, it clearly acknowledges the inaccuracy, corrects the value and uses the corrected data in all subsequent actions. The original entry is not deleted from the log.
After a significant interaction, or at the consumer's request, an AI Receipt is generated from system records rather than from the model's memory. It includes the full conversation, the data and notices received, the operations performed, the final status, the versions of the policy and the passport, details of any human handover and the means of appeal.
Verifiability. The AI Receipt must have a unique number, server time and a means of authenticity verification. The consumer and the company receive matching copies of one and the same record.
8. Complaints, review and changes to the rules
A consumer may dispute incorrect information, an unconfirmed appointment, the use of data or a refusal of human handover. The request should preferably state the date, the name of the service and the AI Receipt number. A complaint is reviewed by a human, and the status and outcome are communicated to the applicant.
Each version of the policy has an effective date. Changes do not retroactively rewrite the rules that applied during a previous interaction. The version archive is kept together with proof of publication.
