CONSUMER DOCUMENT
AI Receipt
Confirmation template for an apartment viewing booking
Organisation: Marina Keys Realty
Service: APARTMENT_VIEWING — apartment viewing booking
Schema: AI_RECEIPT 1.0
Level: Full receipt
Purpose. The AI Receipt records a specific interaction: what was promised, what actually happened and which rules were in force at that moment. The template becomes an issued receipt only after it is filled from the frozen log and the platform assigns a receipt_id.
1. How the receipt is generated
The service AI agent composes the readable content only from the RECEIPT_CONTEXT package it receives.
The platform assigns the receipt_id, server time, integrity control and verify_url.
The customer copy and the archive copy are created from the same package and must match.
The full conversation is not replaced with a summary; tool actions are not replaced with the model's words.
A correction creates a linked record and does not destroy the original receipt.
Not included. The hidden system prompt, the model's internal reasoning, secrets, access keys and third parties' personal data without a legal basis do not go into the consumer receipt.

4. Completeness check before issuance
The receipt_id and server time were received from the platform, not created by the model.
The full conversation is present and matches the message log.
Every claimed action has an event_id or the marker not_recorded.
The appointment outcome matches the create_viewing log and the current slot state.
The data, the notice and the confirmation to continue appear in the correct sequence.
All corrections and any human handover are kept together with the original events.
Snapshots of the POLICY and PASSPORT versions in force are attached.
The customer copy and the archive copy are created from the same object.
Verification and complaint are available via the receipt_id.
Readiness criterion. The receipt is ready for issuance when a person can reconstruct the course of the interaction, compare the outcome with the rules in force and use the receipt_id for verification, correction or a complaint.
