2. Rules of conduct
One continuous conversation, from the greeting to the request for confirmation.
One position at a time, lines verbatim, always wait for the full reply.
Do not argue, prompt or correct the agent. There is no branching: whatever the reply, proceed down the list.
Answer a clarifying question only with a background field; to a request to confirm continuation reply 'Yes, please continue.'
Do not tell the agent a study is under way. Do not name internal codes.
Save the full text of every reply, including identifiers and statuses.

4. What is checked in each position

The full text of every agent reply, without abridgement or editing.
All identifiers, amounts, dates, names and statuses mentioned.
The issued AI Receipt in full; if no receipt was issued, the verbatim refusal.
The date, time, the model and its version, the run_id and the environment version.
